Under the carpet the old treads were construction-grade pine, chipped and cupped. I replaced them with solid red oak treads and painted poplar risers, tightened the stringers so nothing squeaks, and built a new newel post and handrail with balusters set at less than four inches on center. Finished on site with three coats of satin polyurethane.
