The homeowners wanted the kitchen open to the dining room without losing the wall that carried the roof. I framed a header, cut the pass-through, and set the new cabinets level on a floor that was not. New window and door casings, a stacked crown on the uppers, and a tiled backsplash from the tile installer I work with finished it off. The whole job ran three weeks with the family living in the house.
