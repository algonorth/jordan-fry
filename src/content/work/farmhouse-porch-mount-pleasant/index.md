The porch had dropped two inches at the far corner where a footer had failed. We jacked and shored it, poured a new footer, sistered the joists and replaced the rotted rim. The new floor is tongue-and-groove fir, primed on all six sides before it went down, and the ceiling is painted beadboard. The original turned columns were sound, so they were stripped, repaired and repainted rather than replaced.
