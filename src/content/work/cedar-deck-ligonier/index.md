The old deck had a soft frame and a railing you did not want to lean on. We took it down to the ledger, framed new with pressure-treated lumber and proper flashing, and decked it in cedar with hidden fasteners so there are no screw heads to catch a bare foot. The stair is four feet wide with a landing halfway, and the railing is cedar posts with black aluminum balusters so the view of the yard stays open.
