The alcoves on either side of the fireplace were about an inch out of square, which is normal for a house from the fifties. I built the cases in the shop from poplar and birch plywood, scribed the face frames to the walls and ceiling, and ran the new crown into the existing crown so it reads as one piece. Adjustable shelves up top, closed cabinets below, painted to match the trim.
